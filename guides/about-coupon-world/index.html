ABOUT COUPON WORLD

A clearer way to choose a phone.

Coupon World is an independently maintained smartphone research and comparison guide for India. The goal is practical: help you narrow your options using your budget, exact memory requirements and the features that matter to you.

Why the name and product differ

The Coupon World name remains, while our current product focuses on smartphone decisions. The homepage offers phone research, comparison and buying guides. It does not promise a collection of working coupon codes or verified discounts.

What we research

We bring together documented manufacturer specifications, selected independent lab measurements and dated retailer reference information. These sources serve different purposes. Specifications describe published features; lab tests describe a particular device under particular conditions; current offers require separate verification.

This is an independent project, not a claim of affiliation with manufacturers or retailers. We do not claim that we have handled or tested every listed phone. Guides are prepared with AI assistance against saved research. A source link is provided so you can inspect the underlying information.

How updates work

Research is updated in reviewed batches. Cards show research checking dates and independent measurements retain their test context. A checking date means the source was reviewed; it does not mean a lab test, stock check or price measurement happened that day. There is currently no guaranteed automatic refresh schedule.

Where exact variant, region or test compatibility is uncertain, the information remains research context. Missing evidence does not become a zero score, and a related configuration is not silently substituted. Dated price references help filter a budget band; they do not certify today's payable price, stock or delivery.

How the site may earn money

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Tagged Amazon links may earn commission if you make a qualifying purchase, at no extra cost to you. A retailer search link does not certify that a matching listing is in stock. Adding an affiliate tag does not establish program eligibility, commission or a successful order.

Price references are used for budget eligibility, with zero ranking weight. Our current research results do not claim an evidence-backed performance winner where the necessary comparable measurements are missing. Affiliate links do not remove those evidence requirements.

Help correct a record

If a specification appears wrong, note the exact model, RAM/storage, region and source URL. Report the issue through our public repository so the record and supporting evidence can be reviewed. Do not include personal information or order details in a public report.
