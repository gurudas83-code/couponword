**Status:** RESEARCH SELECTION ONLY — no new engine, no product admission, no score/ranking change, no deployment.

## Objective

Focus existing **232** frozen research model cards on **30 specific primary-catalogue identities** for manufacturer-source checking, India RAM/storage validation, comparable independent tests, and answer consistency across static research (Path B) and Render API shopping (Path A).

**Important:** This is *not* a ranked India top-30 sales chart, a current under-₹30,000 price-qualified pool, or 30 verified performance winners. Priority order reflects research workstream grouping rather than performance superiority. All prices require separate dated budget evidence; price contribution to capability score must remain **0%**.

## Selection rationale

- 10 exact catalogue cards from the 6-Nov editorial under-₹25k audit (many other editorial models are absent and remain separate research intake).
- 3 existing catalogue cards cited by Q2 Counterpoint or Q3 Omdia market signals: M17 5G, Galaxy A27 5G, POCO X8 5G. Q3 drivers are not an ordered model sales list; POCO X8 requires India regional-variant confirmation.
- 15 existing candidates informed by the **historical** Google Ads model-demand dataset (Sep 2025–Aug 2026). Historical demand brackets are not current monthly searches or model-sales rankings. Model aliases (e.g. vivo T4X vs T4x 5G) require identity/variant verification.
- 2 Samsung comparison/control models to test existing evidence/fit behavior without inventing winners.

## Release checklist for EACH of the 30

1. Exact Indian product identity and actual RAM/storage variant — no family, model alias, 4G/5G or region substitution.
2. Manufacturer-backed specs with field-level URL, checked date, applicability and conflict/unknown marking.
3. Camera, battery and gaming *independent* tests: publication, exact test unit, firmware, geography, test protocol/version, conditions, observation date. Comparable-cohort gate; manufacturer marketing specs are not benchmark measurements.
4. Reference or dated price only for budget admissibility, **zero weight** in Camera/Gaming/Battery/Overall capability ordering.
5. Exact retailer offer requires matching SKU/variant, approved merchant URL, source date, current stock; until then show plainly labelled retailer **search** destination only.
6. Run all existing identity, budget boundary, 5G maker-source, exclusion, Hindi/Hinglish, research-vs-shopping and ranking-determinism tests. Check Pages deployment and Render `/health` revision independently.
7. Keep `ranking_eligible=false` until existing evidence gates admit a comparable cohort. Never force Top 3 or fake a performance winner.
