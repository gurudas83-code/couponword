# India smartphone search-intent study and 50 comparison publication — 6 Nov 2026

## Search demand sources and interpretation

1. **Historical, not current:** `data/mobile_google_demand_ranked_126.json` contains 126 model research candidates, 122 with model-level historical metrics from the user's archived **Google Ads Keyword Planner** export. Period: **1 Sep 2025–31 Aug 2026**; export dated **12 Sep 2026**. Original source is a local CSV referenced in this JSON. This is a planning snapshot, not a live Google Search Console report. Its numeric brackets and broad model names are **not** validated monthly volumes for each comparison query.
2. **Live directional signal:** Google Trends India's public trending pages on 6 Nov 2026 show active smartphone-related topics, including Apple iPhone 17 and Motorola Signature 27. Trends 'trending now' detects a surge over baseline; it is not the same as long-term, high-volume queries, and not all trending devices have sufficient reviewed source records for indexing.
3. **Independent buyer intent:** Source-backed manufacturer comparison and specification intents are supported by existing `/phones/` research. A question such as '*Galaxy A56 vs Galaxy A36*' is a **proposed buyer-intent query**, not a certified top-50 search-volume result.
4. **Absent data:** There is no authenticated current-November search query export from Google Search Console, no paid contemporary keyword volume set, and no proof that these pair queries are India's fifty most searched. Do not publish numerical pair volumes or rankings.

## What is published and why

- 50 distinct **English** and 50 corresponding **Hindi** buyer-comparison pages, plus two browsable hubs. Each pairing has its own concise editorial decision angle and is not a reordered duplicate.
- Original content derives a sourced side-by-side evidence table from two explicitly reviewed entries in the publisher's 50-model allow-list. Both need at least eight valid manufacturer-linked factual fields; an unreviewed phone is rejected by the build.
- All listed values link to their saved source. Missing/ambiguous data is displayed as unknown. Different camera or processor specifications never imply independently measured superiority.
- Actual real-world gaming, camera and endurance winners, fresh prices, offer freshness and delivery are **not** asserted. Retailer buttons are clearly labelled search/browse links with the site-approved Amazon tag, not product-specific verified checkout.
- `/compare/` and `/hi/compare/` provide static crawlable discovery. Each page includes one canonical URL, reciprocal `hreflang`, sources, related comparison routes, model evidence pages, decision methodology, appropriate structured data and the research date.
- No changes to recommendation scoring, private Product DNA, indexed 232-card research pool, or the **0% performance price weight**. The Google Ads metrics do not change performance recommendations.
- Added regression tests for 50 unique model-pair identities, manufacturer source thresholds, 102 sitemap routes, all internal links, alternate languages, no invented Product/Offer schemas and proper disclosure. Pages launch only on green unit, browser and published-URL checks.

## Editorial gaps and future priority

Publish additional comparisons only where Search Console impressions or genuine buyer questions demonstrate value and where both model records have sufficient original field-level evidence.
