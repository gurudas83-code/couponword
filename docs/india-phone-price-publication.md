# India phone launch and availability rules

Treat the manufacturer’s India announcement and a retailer’s stock observation as separate facts. List upcoming phones only with a manufacturer-confirmed future India release. Keep speculative prices and retailer prices off the upcoming page.

The public price watch displays all 30 tracked phones. A green badge requires an exact retailer API listing, a positive INR price and an in-stock response within 36 hours. When the API evidence is missing, the card shows a price-check link and, if reviewed, a dated public store price reference. Store references are excluded from lowest API price comparisons and do not establish stock. Keep its observed timestamp and PIN visible. The observation applies to that listing and PIN. Confirm actual price, stock and the exact variant at checkout.

A watchlist editorial exclusion overrides historical API prices. Replacements with India product references enter the existing 60-call rotation and appear on the page after passing the stock and price checks.
