# India phone launch and availability rules

Treat the manufacturer’s India announcement and a retailer’s stock observation as separate facts. List upcoming phones only with a manufacturer-confirmed future India release. Keep speculative prices and retailer prices off the upcoming page.

The public price watch displays a tracked phone only after the retailer API returns a matching listing, a positive INR price and an in-stock response within 36 hours. Keep its observed timestamp and PIN visible. The observation applies to that listing and PIN. Confirm actual price, stock and the exact variant at checkout.

A watchlist editorial exclusion overrides historical API prices. Replacements with India product references enter the existing 60-call rotation and appear on the page after passing the stock and price checks.
