# Google-guideline publication review — 6 November 2026

The review applies Google's people-first, spam, generative-content, review,
structured-data and URL-removal guidance. It is an editorial and technical
assessment, not certification by Google or a guarantee of rankings.

## Findings and decisions

- The page-per-query release produced 812 highly templated answer pages and four
  directory/coverage pages. Most did not add a sufficient answer beyond an
  evidence-gap notice and repeated tables. Remove the entire batch, including
  its 24 previously indexable answers; retain the historical queries privately.
- The 100 pair-comparison pages largely repeated a common buying checklist.
  Consolidate into a substantive bilingual comparison guide and the existing
  interactive research tool. Keep the source-linked model pages.
- The 58 under-₹25k model/ranking pages mixed regional specification scope and
  conditional editorial order with “best” headings. One POCO display value was
  6.73 inches rather than 6.67 inches. Retire the batch and retain one carefully
  sourced budget guide in each language, without a tested-winner claim.
- Remove three unreviewed/duplicate legacy SEO pages. The old buying-guide
  inventory listed ready keyword placeholders for missing pages; replace the
  public inventory with the 20 real reviewed guides and remove keyword labels
  from the visitor interface.
- Removed URLs receive actual GitHub Pages 404 responses, with helpful links on
  the custom error page. No blanket home-page redirects or soft-404 placeholders.
- Rebuild the sitemap from retained existing indexable routes. Keep crawling open
  so Google can observe removals and remaining noindex directives. Do not use
  robots.txt as an indexing-removal mechanism.

The retirement manifest lists 977 URLs. Research records, exact variants,
performance scoring and the 0% price weight remain unchanged. Factual
manufacturer research is not presented as first-hand product testing.

## Release requirements

Assert all retired files are absent, every internal static link resolves, every
sitemap URL exists and is indexable/self-canonical, no invented review/rating or
offer schema is emitted, and browser tests pass at 390/768/1440px. Verify real
404 status on production after deployment. Google removal timing depends on
recrawling; publication removal is not instantaneous Search Console removal.
