# Capacity versus endurance

Battery capacity (`mAh`) and measured endurance are separate metric names and separate comparable groups. Capacity may be an official specification; endurance requires compatible independent test protocol, conditions and version. The battery adapter delegates population math to the dynamic benchmark adapter and never derives endurance from mAh.

Current live status: `NO_COMPARABLE_VERIFIED_REFERENCE` for endurance and `LIVE_ELIGIBLE_POOL_EMPTY` for buyable recommendations. No battery-backup leader was invented.
