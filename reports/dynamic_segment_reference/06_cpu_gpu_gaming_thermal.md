# CPU, GPU, gaming and thermal methodology

`python/performance_evidence.py` keeps CPU, GPU, gaming FPS, sustained gaming, frame-time and thermal records separate. Benchmark name/version, workload, game version, graphics preset, resolution and test conditions are required where applicable. Incompatible protocol groups are not merged. Peak FPS alone cannot establish sustained gaming leadership, and chipset identity cannot substitute for measured thermals.

Current live status: no authorized current offer pool and no compatible live performance reference. Gaming and sustained-performance results remain incomplete.
